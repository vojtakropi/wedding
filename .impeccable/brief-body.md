# Surface: wedding invitation + RSVP (public/index.html)

Scope: the whole single page. Visitor mode: Persuade (the guest decides and replies).

Audience and job: Czech family and friends of every age, mostly on phones; learn when/where, decide, reply per guest with dietary needs. Proof/content: the existing Czech copy (story, programme, travel, FAQ, RSVP). Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, RSVP data contract and Apps Script unchanged, Czech typography, older readers.

Chosen direction: Svatební oznámení (user picked the IMPECCABLE'S PICK card over the rolled Maiolica direction, seed 899bb1d0). Memorable moment: the reply slip tears off along its perforation and is stamped "Odesláno".

Unresolved: real names, date, venue, photos (placeholders until the couple supplies them).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the printed Czech wedding announcement (svatební oznámení) unfolded as one long leporelo card, with a perforated reply slip (odpovědní lístek) at its foot. It refuses the template stack of full-bleed couple photo, script names, countdown and detail cards.

OWN-WORLD: Bright cotton card stock with visible fibre on a deep rosemary-green envelope ground; one ink (the same green) printed as letterpress, blind-embossed monogram, engraved Didone type (Bodoni Moda, optical sizes) with Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading and form text. Fold creases divide panels; photos are tipped-in prints with photo corners; controls are printed reply-card boxes and fill-in lines; chosen means ink-filled, empty means printed outline.

STORY: The guest reads the formal announcement, sees the weekend as a printed programme with dotted leaders, finds the villa and how to get there, checks practical answers, then fills in and tears off the reply slip, believing this is the couple's own invitation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Envelope green at the top and sides; the card's front panel fills the column: embossed K&M monogram, small caps "Svatební oznámení", the formula "Klára a Matěj si dovolují oznámit…", names as the largest type on the page in Didone italic, date and place in spaced caps, and at the panel foot a perforated strip asking "Přijedete?" with two printed boxes (S radostí přijedu / Bohužel nemůžu) that start the reply.

FORM: Svatební oznámení with tear-off reply slip; position 1 on my ordered list (shown as the pick card); seed key 899bb1d0. Signature interaction: on a successful reply the slip tears along the perforation, drops away, and the stub is stamped "Odesláno" with the date. Motion grammar: ink-on-paper, quick ease-out presses and ticks, one deliberate tear, nothing looping.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
